# **App Name**: JurisAI

## Core Features:

- AI Chat Assistant: A chat interface where users can ask questions about Indian law and receive answers based on a RAG system against a corpus of legal documents and bare acts. The system uses a tool to reason about when it has enough information to generate an answer.
- Document Upload & Analysis: Users can upload legal documents (PDF, DOCX) for analysis, with features to summarize the document and explore legal terms with AI explanations.
- Law Explorer: A search and browse function for Indian laws (IPC, CrPC, IT Act, etc.), providing readable summaries and related sections, powered by Gemini.
- Past Case Finder: Search past Indian court judgments by keywords or law section, displaying summarized results, powered by Gemini.
- Law Suggestion by Situation: Suggest relevant laws based on user-inputted situations (e.g., theft, cyberbullying).

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep Indigo (#663399) to evoke a sense of authority, trust, and intelligence.
- Background color: Light Lavender (#E6E6FA) a desaturated variant of the primary color, to provide a gentle, unobtrusive backdrop.
- Accent color: Sky Blue (#87CEEB) to add a sense of clarity and accessibility.
- Headline font: 'Playfair', serif, for a modern sans-serif look similar to Didot. Body font: 'PT Sans', sans-serif, for a modern look and readability.
- Use clear and simple icons related to legal concepts. Use a consistent style that aligns with the serious and professional nature of the application.
- One-page layout with a left panel for the AI assistant and a right panel for document upload, preview, and analysis. Implement a responsive design for mobile devices with a top-down layout and collapsible document panel.
- Subtle animations for loading states and transitions to enhance the user experience without being distracting.
